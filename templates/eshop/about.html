O autorke:

Silvia Kormosi je slovenská spisovateľka a autorka série mestského fantasy Tajomstvá svetla, ktorej prvý diel Krištáľové puto vyjde v roku 2026.

Vymýšľaniu postáv a príbehov sa venuje už od útleho veku, no k samotnému písaniu sa naplno dostala až počas materskej dovolenky. Odvtedy s písaním neprestala.

V minulosti sa venovala prekladu a výučbe anglického jazyka, dnes sa sústreďuje najmä na beletriu v žánri YA fantasy.

About the author:

Silvia Kormosi is the author of the The Mysteries of Lights trilogy. The first installment of the series, named the The Crystal Bond, is set to release in 2026.

Silvia has been creating characters and envisioning stories since early childhood, but began writing full-time only during her recent maternity leave. Having discovered a propensity for putting words to paper, she hasn't stopped since.

She previously worked as a translator and English teacher, and now focuses primarily on writing YA fiction. She is based in Slovakia.
